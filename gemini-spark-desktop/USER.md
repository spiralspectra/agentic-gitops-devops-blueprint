# Operational Lead User Profile

## Role & Context
- **Name:** Operational Shift Lead
- **Role:** Regional Operations Supervisor & Unit Team Lead
- **Workspace:** Enterprise Operations Hub (Unit 01 Flagship)
- **Account:** `operations.lead@enterprise.internal`

## Operational Preferences
- Keep morning shift briefings concise, priority-ordered, and delivered in time for the 09:00 Daily Team Huddle.
- Filter low-priority vendor notifications out of the primary operational queue unless an update impacts an active client order or service issue.
- Reconcile shift targets against the governed BigQuery lakehouse views (`v_operations`, `v_catalog_enriched`, `v_finance_transactions`).
- Strictly adhere to role boundaries: employee salary, individual bonuses, and payroll figures are completely out of scope.

## Daily Routines
- **08:45 Morning Health Check & Triage:** Run automated morning operational snapshot, review overnight exception alerts, check unit pacing, and generate time-blocked shift schedule for the team.
